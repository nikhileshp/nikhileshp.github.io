I'm a CS Ph.D. student at the University of Texas at Dallas, working in the [StARLinG Lab](https://starling-lab.github.io/) with Dr. Sriraam Natarajan. I work on reinforcement learning and neurosymbolic AI, with interests in interpretability, learning from human guidance, transfer across environments, and multi-agent systems.

Lately I've been thinking about how much structure an agent can borrow instead of learn: human gaze as a signal for how a task decomposes, world models whose rules carry over from one environment to the next, and asking an expert the right questions when demonstrations are scarce.

Outside the lab I play blitz chess. A Maia2 model fine-tuned on my games lives under [play my chess bot](/chess-clone/) if you want a game.

If you would like to collaborate, please feel free to reach out through [email](mailto:nikhilesh.prabhakar@utdallas.edu).
